One-time setup

  1. Create an API app at strava.com/settings/api.
  2. Set Authorization Callback Domain to localhost.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env and fill in the Client ID and Client Secret.
  4. Restart the server (npm start) and reload this page.

How is the 10th-place time estimated?

Strava no longer lets apps read segment leaderboards, so the 10th-place time is estimated from what the API does expose: the KOM/QOM time and how many athletes have done the segment. Finish times are modelled as log-normally distributed with the chosen spread; the expected gap between 1st and 10th out of N athletes then follows from the normal quantiles. Gender leaderboards assume ~80% of athletes are men and ~20% women.

If fewer than 10 athletes (of your leaderboard) have done a segment, any finish gets you in: those are marked Free. If Strava gave you a kom_rank on one of your efforts, you were top 10 at upload time: marked Top 10. On segments you haven't done, your time is predicted (shown with ~): a model of your speed against grade and distance is fitted to your PRs (needs at least 10 of them per sport), set to a good day (your 75th-percentile effort). Discover in view searches the map view and splits every area that returns Strava's maximum of 10 results into quarters, so it digs past the popular segments to the quiet ones with soft leaderboards.

The estimate is rough. Check the real leaderboard on Strava and enter the actual 10th-place time in the side panel for an exact gap.
